# Code of Conduct

Hydrus Pocket is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project environment.

Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, protect other users' privacy, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or personally identifying content. Personal media collections, credentials, private network details, and private communications must never be posted without explicit permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionately. Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when appropriate.
